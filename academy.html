Why Free?

The AEC industry is full of talented professionals who lack access to modern technical training. University curricula lag behind industry tools, and quality upskilling is expensive. We built the Academy because we've been there — and because a more digitally capable industry means better, safer, more efficient built environments for everyone.

This is our way of giving back.
