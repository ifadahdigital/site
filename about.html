The Legacy Challenge

Most organisations running .NET Framework, WinForms, or WebForms applications face the same dilemma: the systems are too important to touch, but too fragile to ignore.

A full rewrite is expensive, risky, and often fails. But doing nothing means mounting technical debt, security exposure, and lost productivity.

We take a third path: stabilise, extend, and evolve. Using targeted refactoring, test coverage, and incremental modernisation to restore confidence in systems you depend on.

Our Approach
Step 1

Assess

Understand the current state: architecture, dependencies, pain points, and risk areas.

Step 2

Stabilise

Add test coverage, fix critical issues, and document what the system actually does.

Step 3

Modernise Incrementally

Refactor modules, improve performance, and introduce modern patterns, one step at a time.

Step 4

Evolve

Add new capabilities such as integrations, AI features, and modern UI on a now-stable foundation.
